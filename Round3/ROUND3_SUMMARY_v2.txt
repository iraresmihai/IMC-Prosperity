Per-tick mean IV across tradable strikes is extremely stable:

E. SMART BACKTEST  (smartBacktest.py)
------------------------------------------------------------
Linear sizing on flat-IV $-residual:
   target_pos = clip( -K_SIZE * residual_$,  ±POSITION_LIMIT )
+ MIN_TRADE_QTY and RESIDUAL_DEADZONE (only retarget if residual moved
  more than the deadzone since last trade).
Configurable: VOUCHER, SIGMA_FLAT, K_SIZE, deadzone, hedge, cross_spread.
Reports both MTM PnL (market mark) and fair PnL (settlement mark).

VEV_5400, 3 days, deadzone sweep:
              MID exec       CROSS-SPREAD exec
  dz=0.00     +55,881           -291,348
  dz=0.30     +41,607           -145,294
  dz=1.00     + 6,264              -469
  dz=1.50     + 4,602            +  922   <-- best realistic

Lessons:
  - Mean-reversion stat-arb is destroyed by realistic spreads (half-spread
    ~ residual std).
  - Only the structural bias (hold to settlement) survives; ~$1k expected.
  - MTM and fair PnL match within ~$500 -> sigma=0.01262 is a good guess.
