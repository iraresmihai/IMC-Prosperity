Method: invert Black-Scholes per voucher per tick to get sigma_impl,
        plot vs log-moneyness m = log(K/S), fit parabolic smile.
Tradable band: K = 5000..5500 (6 strikes with meaningful time value).
Excluded:
  - K=4000, 4500 (trade AT intrinsic, IV = numerical artifact)
  - K=6000, 6500 (floor-pinned at 0.5, IV also artifact)

Findings:
  * Smile almost flat: a ~ 0.08 on m^2 (real equities usually 0.5-2.0).
    Market is pricing very BS-consistently.
  * Pooled IV ~ 1.25%/day ~ 19.8% annualized.

5. TRADING STRATEGY (volatility stat-arb)
------------------------------------------------------------
Conceptually identical to pairs stat-arb, with BS as the fair-value model.

Loop each tick:
  1. S = mid(VEV)   [mid = avg of highest-VOLUME best bid and best ask]
  2. Fit quadratic smile on tradable strikes (K=5000..5500).
  3. iv_market = BS^-1(C_market, S, K, T)
  4. iv_fit    = smile(log(K/S))
  5. resid = iv_market - iv_fit
  6. z = (resid - rolling_mean) / rolling_std     # over ~1000 ticks
  7. If z < -ENTRY_Z  -> long   POSITION_LIMIT (cheap, buy)
     If z > +ENTRY_Z  -> short -POSITION_LIMIT (rich, sell)
     If |z| < EXIT_Z  -> flat
     Else hold current position.
  8. Delta-hedge: short (delta * voucher_pos) of VEV.
  9. PnL = cash + voucher_pos*voucher_mid + vev_pos*vev_mid

Why this makes money despite never exercising:
  - Market price convergence: the voucher is tradable directly; sell it back
    when it reverts toward fair.
  - End-of-round liquidation at hidden fair value — open positions get
    marked to IMC's "true" fair value, capturing mispricing deterministically.
  - Theta / time value decay — shorting rich extrinsic collects the decay.

------------------------------------------------------------
6. KEY CONVENTION (used across ALL scripts)
------------------------------------------------------------
price = avg(best_bid, best_ask) per timestamp,
        where "best" = the level (1, 2, or 3) with HIGHEST VOLUME
        (NOT necessarily level 1).
Rationale: top-of-book prices with low size are noisier than deeper
levels with real depth. User's consistent definition across all analyses.

10. USEFUL SNIPPETS
------------------------------------------------------------
Black-Scholes inversion, bisection (used everywhere):
  norm_cdf(x) = 0.5 * (1 + erf(x / sqrt(2)))
  bs_call(S, K, T, sigma) uses d1 = (log(S/K) + 0.5*sigma^2*T) / (sigma*sqrt(T))
  implied_vol bisects sigma in [1e-5, 5.0] until BS(S,K,T,sigma) matches market C

TTE convention (days):
  T(day d, ts) = TTE_AT_START[d] - ts / 1_000_000
  where TTE_AT_START = {0: 8.0, 1: 7.0, 2: 6.0}
  Round 3 LIVE starts with TTE = 5.0 days.

Tick grid: timestamp in {0, 100, 200, ..., 999900}  -> 10 000 ticks per day.
